Quick discussion

<h1> identifies this page's main heading, while <h2> introduces a topic beneath it. HTML has six heading levels, from <h1> to <h6>. Choose a level for its place in the outline, rather than its font size.

<p> marks a paragraph. <strong> gives text importance, and <em> gives stress emphasis. Their tags are nested inside the paragraph and close before it closes.
