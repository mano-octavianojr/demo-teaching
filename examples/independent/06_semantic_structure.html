Quick discussion

Semantic elements describe the role of content. <header> introduces the page, <nav> groups major navigation, and <main> holds the primary content. <section> groups a topic, <article> holds a self-contained item, and <footer> provides closing information.

These elements organize meaning; they do not create a styled layout. The document's <head> contains metadata and is different from the visible <header>.
